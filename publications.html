Current Publication & Research Direction

My research investigates how farmers evaluate, accept, and adopt agricultural innovations under economic, informational, behavioral, institutional, and environmental constraints.

Building on my doctoral research at Universiti Putra Malaysia, I examine agricultural extension and advisory systems, farmer decision-making, innovation diffusion, technology acceptance, digital agriculture, smallholder livestock development, and climate-resilient agricultural practices.

My current research agenda places particular emphasis on understanding the transition from willingness to actual adoption, including the roles of communication channels, institutional support, farmer learning, affordability, and access to advisory services.
